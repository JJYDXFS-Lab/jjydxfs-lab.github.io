# Constellation illustrations

These 85 illustrations are by **Johan Meuris**, from the "modern" sky culture of
[Stellarium](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern/illustrations).
They are distributed here unchanged under the [Free Art License 1.3](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/).
The site only tints and scales them when it draws them on the sky.

The anchor stars that pin each illustration to the sky (in [`sky-figures.js`](../../sky-figures.js))
come from the same sky culture's `index.json`, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
